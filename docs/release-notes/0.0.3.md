Foodie gets its own icon and its own app id, and every open feature request is in.

**Foodie now installs as its own app.** It no longer offers to update the old Freezer app; the two install side by side. To move your data, make a backup in Freezer and restore it in Foodie. Older `.freezerbackup` files still restore.

## What's in 0.0.3

**New icon** ([#15](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/pull/15))
The launcher shows a grocery basket.

**Backup and restore no longer hang** ([#4](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/4))
Restoring a backup restarts the app cleanly instead of leaving a spinner on screen, and a backup the system save dialog did not save shows a message.

**Optional backup password** ([#12](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/12))
"Protect with a password" is on by default. Switched off, the backup is saved without encryption after a warning, and restoring it asks for no password.

**Finer slider steps and the usual amount** ([#5](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/5), [#14](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/14))
Taking food out moves in 1 g or ml steps for packages under 50, 10 up to 200 and 50 above that. The amount starts at what you usually take of that product, with a "Usually …" hint.

**Undo time** ([#6](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/6))
Options sets how long the Undo button stays, from 0 to 10 seconds or until the message is closed. The default stays 4 seconds.

**Edit dates** ([#7](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/7))
"Edit dates" in an item's sheet changes the stored-on date and changes or removes the best-before and opened dates.

**Notes in the list** ([#8](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/8))
An item's note shows in brackets after its name, and search finds it.

**Your own word for pieces** ([#9](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/9))
A product counted in pieces can be shown as slices, quarters or any other word. Statistics still count pieces.

**Change a product's unit** ([#10](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/10))
"Change unit…" in the product editor adds the product again in the new unit and archives the old one. Stock, history and learned barcodes are kept.

**Shorter statistics filter** ([#11](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/11))
The product filter lists selected products first, then products with history, with "Show all" for the rest. Search still finds every product.

**Edit a product from its tab** ([#13](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/issues/13))
"Edit product" in an item's sheet opens the product editor.

**Fixed:** saving a product in the editor no longer drops its shelf life after opening.
