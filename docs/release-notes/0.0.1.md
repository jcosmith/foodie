The first release of Foodie: the freezer app becomes a household inventory for the freezer, fridge, pantry and household supplies. Everything still stays on the phone.

The version starts again at 0.0.1 under the new name. Phones with the freezer app 0.1.x update in place and keep their data.

## What's in 0.0.1

**One tab per storage area** ([#2](https://github.com/jcosmith/foodie/pull/2))
Freezer, Fridge and Pantry are tabs of their own, each with its own storage places, products and wording. Household (cleaning, laundry, bathroom, kitchen paper) is one switch away in Options. Switching a tab off keeps its items.

**Lists and More**
Lists holds the shopping list and, when switched on, the receipt archive. More holds Statistics and Options.

**Best-before and opened-on dates**
Items can carry a best-before date and be marked as opened, with a shorter shelf life from then on. "Use soon" and the daily digest use whichever date comes first, down to food that keeps a single day. Shelf lives are entered in days, weeks or months.

**Receipt scanning** (optional, off by default)
Photograph a receipt and the phone reads it, with no network involved. Lines it does not know are flagged for you; the rest are ticked with amount and place, and one tap adds them. Receipts are kept encrypted, card numbers masked, searchable by any word, and what you confirm is learned per store. Older receipt photos can be deleted automatically while their text stays searchable.

**Storage limits per storage area**
Storage limits are grouped by Freezer, Fridge, Pantry and Household, and a limit can be cleared for products that do not go off.
