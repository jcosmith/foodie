Foodie: UI Examples (v3)

Foodie is a household inventory app. Food comes first: the freezer, fridge and pantry, and receipt scanning to add a whole shop at once, all of which are now built. Each storage area is its own tab: Freezer, Fridge and Pantry are on after installing, and Household (cleaning, bathroom, garden and so on) is one switch away in Options, with its basics built early and its full feature set later. "Lists" holds the shopping list and receipts; "More" holds Statistics and Options. Phones 1 to 11 are interactive and show the freezer app of release 0.1.1. Phones 12 to 17 are static sketches of the household screens, which are now built (architecture sections 10.7, 10.8 and 10.10); the app follows them closely.

The same principles hold for every screen: the most frequent actions take one or two taps, optional features disappear completely when switched off, and nothing leaves the phone.

Eleven interactive screen suggestions sharing one in-memory freezer and two years of generated history. Version 2 adds the Config tab (language, appearance, optional features), the freezer layout editor (add, rename, reorder and remove drawers) and a much richer Insights area with a shared filter, cross-filtering and twelve chart types.

The app language is now chosen inside the app, in the Config screen (phone 10), just as users will do it. Dates, numbers and relative ages follow the chosen language through the browser's Intl formatters. The notes under each phone stay in English.

12 · Fridge tab Built

  • Each storage area is its own tab. A new install shows Home, Freezer, Fridge, Pantry, Lists and More; switching Household on in Options adds its tab after Pantry.
  • Every tab has a colour icon in the same style, and each storage area has its own accent colour (Freezer blue, Fridge teal, Pantry amber, Household green) for its icon and section headers, so the tabs are easy to tell apart.
  • Freezer, Fridge, Pantry and Household share one stock screen (search, grouping by storage place, use-first order); only the storage places, products and wording differ.
  • Badges use the earliest of shelf life, best-before and "opened" dates, with colour, icon and word. "Use today" and "Use by tomorrow" cover food that lasts a day or two.

14 · Receipt review Built

  • The text is recognised on the phone. Lines that match no known product are flagged at the top with a short explanation and the choices: pick a product, create one, or ignore it.
  • Likely matches show a question mark and need one tap. Every answer is learned for this store, so the next receipt needs fewer taps.
  • Nothing is added until "Add 12 items"; flagged lines left open stay on the archived receipt.

17 · Options: tabs and optional features Built

  • The switches under "Tabs" add or remove the Freezer, Fridge, Pantry and Household tabs instantly. Freezer, Fridge and Pantry are on after installing; Household is off until the user wants it.
  • Switching a tab off keeps its items; switching it on again brings them back. The last tab that is on cannot be switched off.
  • Receipt scanning, like barcode scanning, is off until the user wants the camera.
